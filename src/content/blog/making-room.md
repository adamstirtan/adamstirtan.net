---
title: Making room for the interesting bitz
date: "2026-04-18"
summary: A note on choosing fewer, more considered things to spend time making.
---

The most useful constraint I know is a little bit of empty space.

When every part of a screen, a week, or a project is already occupied, there is no place for the surprising thing to arrive. Making room is not an absence of effort. It is the effort of deciding what can be left alone.

I am trying to make fewer things that are more particular, and to leave enough margin to notice when they need to change.
