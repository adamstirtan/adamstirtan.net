---
title: A short list of things I keep returning to
date: "2026-02-27"
summary: Notes, tools, and small rituals that keep proving their value.
---

Some things are worth revisiting precisely because they do not ask much of you.

- A notebook that opens flat.
- A walk without a destination.
- A well-made interface with no need to announce itself.
- A song that has room to breathe.

Taste is often less about finding something new than noticing what you continue to reach for.
